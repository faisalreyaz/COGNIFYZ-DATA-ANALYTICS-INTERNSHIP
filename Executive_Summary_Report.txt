KEY ANALYTICAL HIGHLIGHTS (ALL 3 LEVELS — 11 TASKS)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
LEVEL 1:
- Task 1 (Top Cuisines): North Indian leads (3,960 restaurants | 41.46%),
  followed by Chinese (2,735 | 28.64%) and Fast Food (1,986 | 20.79%).
- Task 2 (City Analysis): New Delhi has the highest outlet count (5,473 |
  57.30%); Inner City holds the highest overall average rating (4.90).
- Task 3 (Price Range): Budget dining (Price Range 1) captures 46.53%
  (4,444 restaurants), followed by Price Range 2 at 32.59% (3,113).
- Task 4 (Online Delivery): 25.66% (2,451) offer online delivery and earn
  a higher average rating (3.25) than non-delivery outlets (2.47).

LEVEL 2:
- Task 1 (Ratings): 3.0 - 3.4 is the most common rating tier (2,490
  restaurants | 26.07%); overall average votes per restaurant is 156.91.
- Task 2 (Cuisine Combinations): Standalone "North Indian" (936) and
  "North Indian, Chinese" (511) are the most common combinations.
- Task 3 (Geographic Analysis): India represents 90.59% (8,652 outlets)
  with a dense spatial cluster around Delhi-NCR (28.5°N, 77.2°E).
- Task 4 (Restaurant Chains): 734 multi-outlet chains operate 2,839
  outlets; Cafe Coffee Day leads in count (83) and Barbeque Nation leads
  in total customer votes (28,142 | 4.35 avg rating).

LEVEL 3:
- Task 1 (Review Sentiment & Length): Neutral ("Average" = 3,737) is the
  most frequent sentiment; average text length is 7.02 characters with no
  linear correlation to rating (r = -0.19).
- Task 2 (Votes Analysis): Barbeque Nation holds the brand total record
  (28,142 votes), Toit Bangalore holds the single-outlet record (10,934),
  and 1,094 restaurants have 0 votes. Moderate positive correlation exists
  between votes and rating (r = +0.31).
- Task 3 (Service Availability vs. Price Range): Table booking rises
  sharply with price (0.02% in Tier 1 to 46.76% in Tier 4), while online
  delivery peaks in mid-range dining (41.31% in Tier 2 vs. 9.04% in Tier 4).
