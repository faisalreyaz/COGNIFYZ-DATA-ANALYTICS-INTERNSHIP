# Restaurant Data Analytics | Cognifyz Technologies

**An Excel-Based Data Analytics Internship Project**

## Project Overview

Restaurants generate valuable information about customer preferences, dining costs, ratings, and service availability. This project explores these patterns through structured analysis of the Cognifyz restaurant dataset.

The objective is to transform raw restaurant records into meaningful insights that can support better business understanding and data-driven decisions.

## Project Details

- **Organization:** Cognifyz Technologies
- **Domain:** Data Analysis
- **Dataset:** Restaurant Dataset
- **Records Analyzed:** 9,551
- **Tools:** Microsoft Excel
- **Techniques:** Data Analysis, Pivot Analysis, Dynamic Formulas, Conditional Formatting, Data Visualization

## Analysis Performed

### Level 1 – Restaurant Business Overview

- Identified the most popular cuisines.
- Compared restaurant distribution and ratings across cities.
- Examined restaurant price-range distribution.
- Evaluated online delivery availability and ratings.

### Level 2 – Customer & Market Insights

- Explored aggregate rating patterns and customer votes.
- Identified popular cuisine combinations.
- Examined geographical restaurant distribution.
- Analyzed restaurant chains and their popularity.

### Level 3 – Advanced Restaurant Analytics

- Explored review sentiment and review length.
- Investigated customer voting patterns and rating relationships.
- Compared price ranges with online delivery and table booking services.

## Key Findings

- North Indian cuisine leads the analyzed cuisine categories.
- New Delhi has the highest restaurant count in the dataset.
- Budget-category restaurants account for the largest price-range segment.
- Restaurants offering online delivery show a higher average rating in this analysis.
- Customer votes and restaurant ratings show a moderate positive relationship.
- Table booking availability increases substantially in higher price categories.
